# How to deliver the pitch

One page. The script and cue card are in [PITCH.md](PITCH.md); the Chinese-English version is [PITCH-zh.md](PITCH-zh.md).

## Before your turn (10 minutes)

- Laptop on power, **light mode** on, notifications off, other tabs closed.
- Open `shiftmate.html`, go full screen, zoom 100%. Click **Reset demo** and confirm. Click once on the page.
- Open the hosted link on your phone as a backup, and keep the cue card visible.
- Say the first two sentences and the closing line out loud once. Those are the only lines to memorise word for word.

## On stage

- **First 10 seconds:** look at the judges, not the screen. Say: "Our tutor works a five-hour shift."
- **Say it, then click, then pause.** Tell them what's next, click, and wait one second so they can read the screen.
- **Point with the mouse** at what you're talking about: the W chip, the C chip, "Why was it left blank?", "~65 min". Then move the cursor away.
- **Use the left menu** for every screen change. You never need to scroll to find a button.
- **Pace:** short sentences, slower than feels natural. Stress the numbers and the key words: *three spreadsheets*, *W*, *C*, *65 minutes*, *the tutor decides*.
- **If you lose your place,** glance at the cue card's Key words column and carry on from the next row. Don't apologise.

## Q&A (2 minutes)

- Repeat or rephrase the question so everyone hears it, and so you get two seconds to think.
- Answer in one sentence, add one detail, then stop.
- If a screen helps, show it: Impact for numbers and safety, the inbox's "Privacy: what left the centre" for data questions.
- If you don't know, say so and say what the pilot would test. Never guess a number.
- Be honest about limits: the AI path and the Sheets add-on are built and tested with stand-ins, not yet run live, and the 65 minutes is an estimate.

## If something breaks

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Screen looks wrong | **Reset demo**, then jump with the left menu to where you were. |
| Reset dialog pops up again | Press Esc. |
| Laptop or projector dies | Phone backup with the hosted link; narrate the same steps. |
| Running long | Skip the Daily report. |

## Rehearsal plan (with the time you have)

1. **Run 1:** read the script aloud with the demo, timing it on your phone. Mark where you stumble.
2. **Run 2:** cue card only. Aim for 2:45-2:55.
3. **Run 3:** a friend asks three Q&A questions from PITCH.md; answer in one sentence each.
4. **Before you go on:** reset the demo, then leave the laptop on the Overview page.
